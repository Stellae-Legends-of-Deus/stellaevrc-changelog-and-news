**Welcome, traveler.**

Stellae: Legends of Deus now has a home beyond the world itself. Everything about Stellae lives here: what's new, what changed, the lore behind it all, and anything that breaks along the way.

---

## What you'll find here

### News
Announcements, events and updates from the team land here first. You're reading the very first one.

### Map changelogs
Every map keeps its own changelog, and every build gets an entry. If something was added, moved or fixed, it's written down.

### The wiki
Guides and lore, gathered in one place and growing alongside the world. New to Stellae? Start there.

### Bug reports
Something broken? Sign in with your **VRChat Legends** account and tell us about it. Your reports go straight to the people fixing things.
