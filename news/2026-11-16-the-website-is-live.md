The Stellae: Legends of Deus website is up.

- **News** like this post lives here.
- **Changelogs** for each map list what changed in every build.
- **The wiki** collects guides and lore as the world grows.
- **Bug reports**: sign in with your VRChat Legends account and report anything that breaks.

Custom art for the site is on the way. Until then, enjoy the stars.
